# NVIDIA Financial Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This analysis examines NVIDIA's financial performance across FY2023–FY2026, focusing on revenue growth, profitability, gross profit estimates, and net income trends.

All financial figures are expressed in **USD millions**, unless otherwise stated.

The analysis uses GAAP financial figures from NVIDIA's annual reports.

## 2. Financial Performance Dataset

| Fiscal Year | Revenue | Operating Income | Net Income |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FY2023 | 26,974 | 4,224 | 4,368 |
| FY2024 | 60,922 | 32,972 | 29,760 |
| FY2025 | 130,497 | 81,453 | 72,880 |
| FY2026 | 215,938 | 130,387 | 120,067 |

## 3. Revenue Growth Analysis

| Fiscal Year | Revenue Growth |
|---|---:|
| FY2024 | 125.85% |
| FY2025 | 114.20% |
| FY2026 | 65.47% |

**Finding:** NVIDIA's revenue increased substantially throughout the period. However, its year-over-year growth rate moderated from FY2024 to FY2026.

## 4. Profitability Analysis

| Fiscal Year | Gross Margin | Operating Margin | Net Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FY2023 | 56.90% | 15.66% | 16.19% |
| FY2024 | 72.70% | 54.12% | 48.85% |
| FY2025 | 75.00% | 62.42% | 55.85% |
| FY2026 | 71.10% | 60.38% | 55.60% |

**Findings:**
- Gross margin peaked at 75.00% in FY2025 before moderating in FY2026.
- Operating margin increased significantly between FY2023 and FY2025.
- Net profit margin also expanded substantially and remained above 55% in FY2025 and FY2026.

## 5. Estimated Gross Profit

Estimated gross profit is calculated using revenue and the reported gross margin.

| Fiscal Year | Estimated Gross Profit (USD millions) |
|---|---:|
| FY2023 | 15,348.21 |
| FY2024 | 44,290.29 |
| FY2025 | 97,872.75 |
| FY2026 | 153,531.92 |

These are estimates because the reported gross margins are rounded to one decimal place.

## 6. Net Income Growth

| Fiscal Year | Net Income Growth |
|---|---:|
| FY2024 | 581.32% |
| FY2025 | 144.89% |
| FY2026 | 64.75% |

Net income growth was particularly strong in FY2024. Growth subsequently moderated, reaching 64.75% in FY2026.

## 7. Key Business Observations

1. NVIDIA experienced substantial revenue expansion across the four fiscal years.
2. Profitability improved significantly between FY2023 and FY2025.
3. Revenue and net income continued to increase in FY2026, despite slower growth rates.
4. Gross, operating, and net profit margins eased slightly in FY2026 compared with FY2025.
5. Revenue and net income growth rates moderated over the period, although both remained positive.

These observations describe historical financial performance and should not be interpreted as forecasts.

## 8. Methodology and Limitations

- Revenue growth = (Current-year revenue / Previous-year revenue) - 1.
- Operating margin = Operating income / Revenue.
- Net profit margin = Net income / Revenue.
- Estimated gross profit = Revenue × Reported gross margin.
- Gross profit estimates may differ slightly from exact reported gross profit because the displayed gross margins are rounded.
- FY2023 growth comparisons are omitted because FY2022 is outside this dataset.
- Fiscal years are not equivalent to calendar years.
- Historical financial performance does not guarantee future results.
